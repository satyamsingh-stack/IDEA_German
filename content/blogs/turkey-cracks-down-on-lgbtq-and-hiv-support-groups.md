---
title: " Turkey Cracks Down on LGBTQ+ and HIV Support Groups"
description: >
  
  Turkish authorities have launched coordinated raids against LGBTQ+ rights and HIV support organisations in several cities, including Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir. The investigation reportedly involves 162 suspects, nine associations and 13 businesses, with dozens of people detained and some organisations’ websites blocked. Authorities said the operation was aimed at protecting children, families and public order.


  Human rights groups have condemned the raids, arguing that they represent a broader crackdown on LGBTQ+ people and civil society. The developments come amid increasing restrictions on LGBTQ+ advocacy in Turkey, where Istanbul Pride has been banned since 2015. Although same-sex relations are legal in Turkey, LGBTQ+ organisations and individuals continue to face discrimination and restrictions on public advocacy.
