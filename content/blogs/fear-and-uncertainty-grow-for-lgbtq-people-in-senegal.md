title: Fear and Uncertainty Grow for LGBTQ+ People in Senegal
description: >
  Senegal's LGBTQ+ community is facing increasing fear after the country
  strengthened its anti-homosexuality law in March 2026. The revised legislation
  raises prison sentences for same-sex relations to between five and ten years,
  prompting many people to go into hiding or leave the country. According to
  recent reporting by *Le Monde*, some LGBTQ+ individuals now live with the
  constant fear of arrest, relying on encrypted messaging apps, changing meeting
  locations, and avoiding public attention. Others have reportedly been detained
  or forced to flee to neighbouring countries. Human rights advocates warn that
  the tougher law has created an atmosphere of anxiety and isolation, making it
  even harder for LGBTQ+ people to live openly or safely. The situation has
  renewed international concern about the protection of LGBTQ+ rights and
  freedoms in Senegal. As the debate over human rights continues worldwide, the
  experiences of Senegal's LGBTQ+ community highlight the real-life impact that
  legal and political changes can have on vulnerable groups.
