## Executive Summary

This article explores the critical intersection of migration law and LGBTQ+ rights, examining how asylum systems address claims based on persecution related to sexual orientation and gender identity.

## Persecution on Grounds of SOGI

### Legal recognition
Many jurisdictions recognize persecution based on sexual orientation and gender identity (SOGI) as grounds for asylum. However, implementation varies significantly.

### Evidence and credibility
Applicants often face challenges in providing sufficient evidence of persecution and establishing credibility before asylum authorities.

### Regional variations
Different regions apply different standards and interpretations of what constitutes persecution on SOGI grounds.

## Best Practices in Asylum Adjudication

- Trauma-informed interview approaches
- Recognition of cultural contexts and hidden persecution
- Adequate legal representation
- Non-discriminatory assessment procedures

## Recommendations

National asylum systems should adopt explicit protections for LGBTQ+ asylum seekers, including training for adjudicators and specialized legal pathways.
