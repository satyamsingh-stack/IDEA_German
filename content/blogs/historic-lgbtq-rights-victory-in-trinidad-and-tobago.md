title: Historic LGBTQ+ Rights Victory in Trinidad and Tobago
description: >
  In a landmark ruling, the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council in London
  has overturned colonial-era laws criminalising consensual same-sex intimacy in
  Trinidad and Tobago. The unanimous decision follows an 11-year legal battle
  led by LGBTQ+ activist Jason Jones, 62, and recognises the fundamental right
  to privacy and family life. This historic judgment marks a major step towards
  LGBTQ+ equality and could inspire similar legal challenges across Commonwealth
  countries where same-sex intimacy remains criminalised.
