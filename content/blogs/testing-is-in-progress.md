  artificial intelligence that enables computers to create new and original
  content, such as text, images, videos, music, computer code, and other forms
  of digital media, based on user instructions or prompts. Unlike traditional AI
  systems, which are mainly designed to analyze data, recognize patterns, or
  make predictions, Generative AI can produce creative outputs that closely
  resemble human-generated work. It is powered by advanced machine learning
  models trained on large amounts of data, allowing it to understand language,
  context, and patterns. Today, Generative AI is widely used in education,
  healthcare, business, software development, marketing, customer service, and
  entertainment to improve productivity, automate tasks, support
  decision-making, and enhance creativity.
