title: Rising Concerns Over LGBTQ+ Rights in Niger
description: >
  Niger has come under international scrutiny following the introduction of new
  laws criminalizing same-sex relationships. Human rights organizations report
  that dozens of people have been arrested since the legislation came into
  effect, creating an atmosphere of fear among LGBTQ+ communities. Critics
  describe the crackdown as a serious setback for human rights, with many
  individuals reportedly going into hiding to avoid arrest. The situation has
  also affected public health efforts, as organizations providing HIV prevention
  services have suspended their activities. Advocates warn that restricting
  these services could reverse years of progress in combating HIV AIDS. The
  developments in Niger reflect a broader trend in parts of Africa, where
  several countries have recently adopted stricter laws targeting LGBTQ+ people.
  The debate continues as human rights groups call on authorities to reconsider
  the legislation and protect the rights and wellbeing of all citizens.
