title: Oklahoma Extends Restrictions on Transgender Healthcare
description: "Oklahoma has expanded restrictions on gender-affirming healthcare
  through Senate Bill 904, signed into law on May 12, 2026.      The law
  prohibits the use of public funds and state facilities for gender-transition
  procedures for both minors and adults       and prevents the state Medicaid
  program from covering such care. The measure includes hormone treatments,
  puberty blockers and   certain surgical procedures. It has affected
  transgender adults receiving care through state-linked health systems, with
  some    patients having to seek treatment from private providers instead. The
  law marks a significant expansion of Oklahoma's previous   restrictions, which
  had focused primarily on gender-affirming medical care for minors. "
date: 2026-09-23
