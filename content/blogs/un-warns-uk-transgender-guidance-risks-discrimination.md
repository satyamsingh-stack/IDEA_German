title: UN Warns UK Transgender Guidance Risks Discrimination
description: >
  UN human rights experts have warned that the UK's revised guidance under the
  Equality Act 2010 could increase discrimination against transgender people.
  The new Code of Practice, in force since 5 August 2026, states that for the
  purposes of the Act, a person's sex remains their biological sex regardless of
  whether they hold a Gender Recognition Certificate. This could allow
  transgender women to be excluded from women-only services and transgender men
  from men-only services. UN experts fear the guidance could make exclusion
  appear to be a means of complying with equality law and potentially
  institutionalise discrimination. The Good Law Project is supporting a legal
  challenge to the Code. The UN has urged the UK to ensure that the rights of
  women and girls are protected while equally safeguarding transgender,
  gender-diverse and intersex people from discrimination.
date: 2026-08-19
