---
title: "Hungry Moves to Roll Back Anti-LGBTQ+ laws "
description: >
  Hungary’s new government has submitted a bill to parliament that
  would remove key restrictions on LGBTQ+ content introduced under former Prime
  Minister Viktor Orbán in 2021. Those rules prohibited the portrayal or
  “promotion” of homosexuality and gender transition in media and other content
  accessible to minors. The proposed legislation follows an April ruling by the
  EU’s top court that Hungary’s restrictions violated EU law and contributed to
  the stigmatisation and marginalisation of LGBTQ+ people. The new rules would
  instead focus child-protection measures on harmful or age-inappropriate sexual
  content, exploitation and abuse. Prime Minister Péter Magyar has also said his
  government plans to change rules that have effectively restricted adoption by
  gay people. However, some LGBTQ+ rights groups say further reforms are still
  needed because other restrictive provisions remain in Hungarian law.
date: 2026-09-23
