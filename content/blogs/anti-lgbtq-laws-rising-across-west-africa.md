title: Anti-LGBTQ+ Laws Rising Across West Africa
description: > 
"LGBTQ+ rights are facing a growing crackdown across West Africa,
with several countries introducing or strengthening laws criminalising
same-sex relationships and LGBTQ+ activities. Senegal has increased the
maximum prison sentence for same-sex relations to 10 years, while Niger, Mali
and Burkina Faso have recently criminalised same-sex intimacy. Ghana's
parliament has also passed legislation targeting LGBTQ+ activities. Human
rights activists warn that LGBTQ+ people are increasingly being used as
political scapegoats, while religious and international conservative movements
are also influencing the debate. The consequences extend beyond
discrimination: activists report arrests, violence and growing difficulties
accessing essential services, including HIV prevention and treatment."
