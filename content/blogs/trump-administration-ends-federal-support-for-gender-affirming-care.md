---
title: Trump Administration Ends Federal Support for Gender-Affirming Care
description: >
  The Trump administration has finalized a rule ending federal funding for
  gender-affirming care for children through Medicaid and CHIP. The measure is
  scheduled to take effect on 13 October 2026, although legal challenges could
  delay or block it. LGBTQQ+ and healthcare advocates warn that the policy will
  make treatment particularly difficult to access for vulnerable and low-income
  transgender youth. Supporters of the administration's policy argue that
  federal funds should only support treatments with sufficient evidence of
  safety and long-term benefit. The measure is part of a broader political and
  legal battle over transgender healthcare in the United States. 
date: 2026-08-19
